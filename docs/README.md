# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

## Overview

OctoAcme uses a customer-first, iterative approach to project management. These documents provide shared guidance for taking work from initial idea through delivery and continuous improvement, with clear ownership, transparent communication, and measurable outcomes.

## Project Management Processes

OctoAcme follows an iterative lifecycle. During **initiation**, the team validates the business need, identifies stakeholders, defines success criteria, and decides whether to proceed. **Planning** turns approved work into a prioritized backlog with acceptance criteria, estimates, dependencies, milestones, and a Definition of Done. During **execution and tracking**, the team delivers in small increments using a transparent project board, regular check-ins, testing, and pull request reviews. Risks and dependencies are monitored and escalated through regular stakeholder communication. **Release and deployment** follows pre-release quality checks, smoke testing, deployment verification, and rollback planning. After each sprint, release, or milestone, **retrospectives** capture learnings and turn them into owned, tracked improvement actions.
